## DuoLikeAnimation

`Sources/Duo/Shaders.swift` follows the frosted-glass model published in
[DuoLikeAnimation](https://github.com/elijah-semyonov/DuoLikeAnimation): an eye that stays
put, a ray cast per pixel, and a blur whose radius — along with the light it swallows —
grows with the gap between the screen and the surface the ray lands on. Duo
re-implements it in Metal for a laptop lid rather than a phone in the hand.

## DuoBar

The glyph in `Sources/Duo/Widgets/DuoGlyphs.swift` is
[DuoBar](https://github.com/Mikeli7666/DuoBar)'s, redrawn from its measurements: the ring
open at the bottom with its stroke and offsets, the three-band Wi-Fi mark and its curves,
the bolt and where it sits, the four volume dots and their spacing, the centre symbols
and the headset colour. Duo shows it beside the Dock rather than in the menu bar.
